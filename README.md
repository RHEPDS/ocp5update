# OpenShift 5 Technical Overview Workshop

OpenShift 5 is the next step in Red Hat's enterprise platform strategy, bringing together a RHEL 10 foundation, stronger long-term data protection, AI-assisted operations, streamlined lifecycle management, and closer integration between containers and virtual machines.

This repository contains materials for an interactive, three-hour technical overview workshop designed to help participants understand what these changes mean for platform teams, security leaders, and application developers.

## Workshop goal

Enable participants to assess how OpenShift 5 can advance their platform strategy through:

- The RHEL 10 foundation
- Quantum-safe security
- AI-assisted operations with OpenShift Lightspeed
- Streamlined lifecycle management
- Modernized virtualization

## Topics covered

### RHEL 10 foundation and security

The workshop introduces the new RHEL 10 foundation and examines how post-quantum, or quantum-safe, cryptography can help organizations prepare for future security requirements.

### OpenShift Lightspeed

Participants explore how OpenShift Lightspeed supports platform operations through contextual AI recommendations, analysis, troubleshooting, and controlled execution of changes with approvals, traceability, and governance guardrails.

### Lifecycle management

The session discusses simplified update paths and extended support options, with a focus on balancing modernization, availability, and compliance.

### OpenShift Virtualization

The workshop reviews how virtual machines, containers, and modern cloud-native workloads can be operated, modernized, and evolved gradually on a common platform.

## Intended audience

- Technical Sales and PreSales professionals
- Business consultants
- Delivery consultants

## Prerequisites

Participants should have:

- OpenShift knowledge equivalent to DO180
- A basic understanding of OpenShift Virtualization
- Previous participation in an OpenShift Architecture Workshop

## Agenda

The workshop runs for three hours:

1. **Welcome and introduction** - 45 minutes
2. **OpenShift 5 technical overview** - 45 minutes
3. **OpenShift Lightspeed** - 30 minutes
4. **Enhanced security features** - 30 minutes
5. **OpenShift Virtualization in OpenShift 5** - 20 minutes
6. **Q&A and upcoming training sessions** - 10 minutes

## Expected outcome

By the end of the workshop, participants will have a prioritized, actionable outline for their OpenShift 5 journey, including:

- Relevant security considerations and cryptographic future-readiness
- Candidate operational use cases for OpenShift Lightspeed
- An update and support approach
- Next steps for integrating virtual machines and cloud-native workloads

The session combines expert input, live discussion, and practical scenarios so that participants leave with guidance they can apply to their own OpenShift roadmap.
